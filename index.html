Hi! I'm Sam, and I teach private music & digital media lessons out of my home studio near the Columbia Slough.

I've spent more than 10 years helping students go from "I have no idea what I'm doing" to excited and proud of what they've made — whether that's on an instrument or behind a screen.

I don't teach out of books or for recitals — instead I follow the interests of each student, and provide structure and guidance for whatever path they choose. It's a choose your own adventure! Poke around below and find your path.
