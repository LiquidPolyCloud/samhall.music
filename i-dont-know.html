I Don't Know!

Why not try a bit of everything?

Book Lessons

Maybe you're not sure what you want. Or maybe you're a young one who doesn't know what all this is about. Worry not!

As a multi-instrumentalist and multimedia artist, I can help. I offer a structure of lessons that sets out to do one thing above all else: get you experience with lots of different things. That way, you can try it all, and then choose on your own what you'd like to put your time and energy into.

We'll spend 4 lessons (flexible with goals) with each instrument or digital media tool. We can do as many or as few as you like, and hopefully we find something that speaks to you!

RECOMMENDED

  • Try at least 3 different instruments or digital media tools.
